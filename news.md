<p>In collaboration with experimental groups, we report highly anisotropic charge dynamics in the trilayer nickelate La<sub>4</sub>Ni<sub>3</sub>O<sub>10</sub>, revealing distinct behavior that sheds light on the electronic structure of this recently discovered material family.</p>

<details>
<summary><strong>Aug 2026</strong> — "Strongly Enhanced Charge-Density Waves and Correlated Insulating State in Atomically Thin 1<em>T</em>-TaS<sub>2</sub>" published in <a href="https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/pxpf-6bsv"><em>Phys. Rev. Lett.</em> 137, 066502 (2026)</a></summary>
<p>In collaboration with experimental groups, we show that the charge-density-wave order and the associated correlated insulating state of 1<em>T</em>-TaS<sub>2</sub> are strongly enhanced upon approaching the two-dimensional limit. Our calculations trace this enhancement to strengthened Coulomb interactions arising from reduced out-of-plane screening.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Jun 2026</strong> — "<em>d</em>-wave altermagnetism revealed by resonant inelastic X-ray scattering" — new preprint on <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18731">arXiv:2606.18731</a></summary>
<p>Together with experimental collaborators, we provide direct evidence for <em>d</em>-wave altermagnetism in La<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub>Mn<sub>2</sub>Se<sub>2</sub> using circularly polarized RIXS. The observed dichroism is a direct consequence of altermagnetic symmetry constraints, establishing RIXS as a probe of magnetic phases inaccessible to conventional techniques.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Jun 2026</strong> — "Probing <em>d</em>-wave superconducting gap of the high-<em>T</em><sub>c</sub> cuprate Bi<sub>2</sub>Sr<sub>2</sub>Ca<sub>2</sub>Cu<sub>3</sub>O<sub>10+δ</sub> by resonant inelastic x-ray scattering" published in <a href="https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/4tm5-f5mj"><em>Phys. Rev. B</em> 113, 224510 (2026)</a></summary>
<p>High-resolution RIXS at the Cu <em>L</em><sub>3</sub> edge reveals the formation of the superconducting gap in the overdoped cuprate Bi<sub>2</sub>Sr<sub>2</sub>Ca<sub>2</sub>Cu<sub>3</sub>O<sub>10+δ</sub>. Comparison with our calculations of the momentum-dependent charge susceptibility supports a <em>d</em>-wave symmetry of the gap.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>May 2026</strong> — "Distinct orbital contributions to electronic and magnetic structures in La<sub>4</sub>Ni<sub>3</sub>O<sub>10</sub>" published in <a href="https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/abstract/10.1103/7ds6-z5w8"><em>Phys. Rev. Research</em> 8, 023119 (2026)</a></summary>
<p>Combining X-ray spectroscopies with theory, we resolve the distinct orbital contributions to the electronic and magnetic structure of the trilayer nickelate La<sub>4</sub>Ni<sub>3</sub>O<sub>10</sub>, showing that the more itinerant <em>d</em><sub>x²−y²</sub> states dominate the low-energy charge excitations.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Apr 2026</strong> — "3<em>d</em><sub>z²</sub> orbital delocalization and magnetic collapse in superconducting (La,Pr)<sub>3</sub>Ni<sub>2</sub>O<sub>7−δ</sub> films" — new preprint on <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21899">arXiv:2604.21899</a></summary>
<p>In collaboration with experimental groups, we track how superconductivity emerges in (La,Pr)<sub>3</sub>Ni<sub>2</sub>O<sub>7−δ</sub> thin films as strain and oxygen content are tuned independently: the Ni 3<em>d</em><sub>z²</sub> orbitals become increasingly itinerant while long-range spin-density-wave order collapses, pointing to orbital delocalization and the loss of magnetic coherence as the route to superconductivity.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Apr 2026</strong> — "Interlayer hybridization enables superconductivity in bilayer nickelates" — new preprint on <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14701">arXiv:2604.14701</a></summary>
<p>RIXS measurements on superconducting bilayer nickelate thin films show that the in-plane <em>d</em><sub>x²−y²</sub> states form an itinerant backbone, while superconductivity emerges only when coherent <em>d</em><sub>z²</sub>–<em>p</em><sub>z</sub>–<em>d</em><sub>z²</sub> interlayer hybridization develops.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Apr 2026</strong> — "Dissecting superconductivity in the Ruddlesden-Popper nickelates: The role of electron correlation and interlayer magnetic exchange" — new preprint on <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01902">arXiv:2604.01902</a></summary>
<p>A comparative RIXS study of the trilayer nickelate La<sub>4</sub>Ni<sub>3</sub>O<sub>10</sub> and its bilayer counterpart reveals weaker electronic correlations and a reduced interlayer magnetic exchange in the trilayer compound, which together account for its substantially lower superconducting transition temperature.</p>
</details>
